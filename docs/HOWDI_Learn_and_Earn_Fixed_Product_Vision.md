# HOWDI Learn & Earn

**Fixed Product Vision & Architecture • V1.0 • 11 September 2026**

> **Learn → Practice → Prove → Create → Earn → Grow**

## 1. Product Definition
HOWDI Learn & Earn is not a conventional LMS. It is a Skill-to-Life system that helps a person choose a goal, learn in the easiest useful way, prove the skill through real work, and connect the skill to income, commerce, services, teaching or employment.

Core rule: intelligence stays behind the screen. The learner sees the easiest next action, not platform complexity.

- Learner: Tell me only what I need to do next.
- Teacher: Let me teach; HOWDI handles routine administration.
- Admin: Give me control, quality visibility and auditability without complicating the learner experience.
- AI: Assist, simplify, translate and personalize; never pretend uncertain guidance is correct.
- HOWDI: Connect verified skills to real opportunities.

## 2. Identity & Login
Learn & Earn reuses the existing HOWDI identity. No separate learning account.

- Existing HOWDI login / identity.
- Mobile + OTP where applicable.
- Passkey.
- Mobile screen-lock/device sign-in.
- Gmail sign-in.
- User-selected Login ID + password.
- One identity across Shop, Works, Vendor, Learn & Earn, HPay and future services.

## 3. Audience-First UX
Design for homemakers, Tier-1/Tier-2/Tier-3 users, first-time digital learners, regional-language users, limited daily time and unstable connectivity.

### One-Button Learning
At most learner states, expose one primary action: **CONTINUE**. HOWDI decides whether it means recorded clip, live class, practice, worksheet, project upload, feedback or revision.

### No-App-Knowledge Assistance
- Detect/ask digital comfort and simplify automatically.
- Large primary actions and minimal navigation.
- Voice-first help and read-aloud.
- Camera-first “Show HOWDI” submissions.
- Slow replay, show again, larger text and image-led instructions.
- Low-data video, audio-only, text+image and PDF alternatives.
- Resume exactly where the learner stopped.

### Daily Skill Companion
HOWDI prepares a small daily journey based on available time: Watch → Practice → Show → Grandma Tip. The learner does not need to understand course architecture.

## 4. Goal-First Onboarding
Ask “What do you want to achieve?” rather than “Which course?”

- Hobby / personal learning.
- Earn from home.
- Make and sell products.
- Offer a service.
- Get a job.
- Start a small business.
- Become a teacher/mentor.

HOWDI then builds a Skill Route.

## 5. Economic Pathways
- **LEARN → MAKE → SELL** — creators connect to HOWDI Shop.
- **LEARN → SERVE → EARN** — service skills connect to HOWDI Works.
- **LEARN → GET JOB** — job skills connect to future opportunity/employment workflows.

## 7. PDF, Worksheets & Print-and-Learn
PDF is a first-class format: crochet worksheets, printable patterns, stitch charts, material lists, class-readiness checklists, project guides, homework, assessments, job-prep sheets, resume templates and supporting evidence. Provide large one-page visual Print & Learn instructions where useful.

## 8. Class Readiness & Practice Kits
Before live learning, HOWDI should check required materials, pre-class clips, practice tasks and device readiness.

- Hook size, yarn type/quantity and accessories.
- Rough/practice crochet material or swatch.
- Pre-class micro-videos.
- Mic/speaker/network test.
- I HAVE MATERIALS / BUY PRACTICE KIT / NEED HELP.
- Optional HOWDI Shop course-kit linkage.
- Readiness reminders before first class.

## 9. Practical Learning Loop
**Watch → Try → Show → Help → Retry → Complete.** Short explanation should be followed quickly by hand practice.

### Mistake Library
Store correct examples plus common mistakes and targeted rescue instructions.

### Watch My Hands
Future camera/video assistance may identify likely visible issues. When confidence is insufficient, HOWDI must say so and escalate to a teacher or request a better image/video.

### Emotional Progress
Show what the learner can now do rather than only a percentage. Preserve meaningful “I Can Do This” milestones in My Skill Story.

## 10. Grandma Experience
- Grandma Tip and Grandma Says.
- Slow, warm demonstrations.
- My Crochet Diary / Skill Story.
- Family learning.
- Grandma Memory: preserve family craft voice notes, patterns, stories and demonstrations.

Warmth must never reduce instructional accuracy.

## 11. Dedicated Teacher Portal
Teacher top-level experience: Today, My Schedule, Learners, Batches, Classes, Courses, AI Studio, Messages, Performance, Earnings and My Profile.

Teacher onboarding: Register → Teacher HOWDI ID → profile → languages → skills → experience → demo teaching video → KYC → bank/HPay → Admin verification → Approved Teacher.

Teacher Daily Brief should surface today's classes, learners needing attention, missed practice, voice-help requests and prepared lesson context.

### Teacher vs HOWDI
Teacher provides skill knowledge, demonstrations, judgment, human feedback, live teaching, correction, project evaluation and mentorship. HOWDI provides templates, content delivery, scheduling, matching, submissions, reminders, progress tracking, AI assistance, translations, attendance, certificates and HPay records.

## 12. Scheduling, Demo, Group & 1:1
- Teacher availability and bookable slots.
- Demo, 1:1, Group, Workshop and Doubt Session types.
- Demo request → matching → readiness → session → outcome → next recommendation.
- Batch capacity, recurrence, language, level and teacher.
- Batch enrollment/waitlist.
- Learner slot selection and rescheduling.
- Live class Join card with readiness/device tests.
- Attendance suggested from join/leave duration, then teacher confirmation.

## 13. Smart Matching
Recommend teachers/batches using skill level, language, preferred time, learning mode, availability, rating, batch capacity and relevant learner requirements. HOWDI can suggest a new batch when enough compatible learners exist; teacher/Admin confirms.

## 14. Teacher AI Copilot & AI Clip Studio
AI creation belongs primarily in Teacher/Admin workflows. Teacher specifies skill, level, language, common difficulty, duration and style; HOWDI prepares script/storyboard/clip; teacher previews, edits and approves before learner publication.

Clip styles: hands-only process, animated stitch explanation, Grandma-style guidance, correct-vs-incorrect comparison, slow motion/arrows, mistake rescue and recap.

Teacher Copilot can help create lesson plans, practice, quizzes, Grandma Tips, translations, subtitles, worksheets/PDFs, AI clips, learner-difficulty summaries, homework and batch messages.

AI-assisted lessons must be labelled. Future cloning of a real teacher's likeness/voice requires explicit permission.

## 15. Curriculum Templates
Teachers should not need to be instructional designers. HOWDI can propose: Introduction → Materials → Demonstration → Grandma Tip → Practice → Common Mistake → Checkpoint → Practice Upload. Teacher accepts or edits.

## 16. Community Learning
Support small language/level Learning Circles, optional Skill Buddies, family learning, SHG/community batches and institutional/community programs with attendance, assessment, certification and product onboarding.

## 17. Skill Passport & Proof-of-Skill
Build living proof beyond a PDF certificate: course completion, teacher verification, assessment score, project evidence, multiple finished pieces and future product/service evidence.

Course output types: `HOBBY`, `SKILL`, `PRODUCT_CREATION`, `SERVICE_READY`, `JOB_READY`, `BUSINESS_READY`, `TEACHER_READY`.

## 18. Skill → Opportunity Engine
After proving a skill, ask the learner whether they want to sell, offer a service, find jobs, become a mentor, learn advanced skills or continue practicing.

Future Shop/Works demand signals can recommend useful skills, creating **Demand → Learn → Supply**. Micro-earn milestones can unlock useful/sellable outcomes before a long curriculum is fully completed where appropriate.

## 19. Job Seeker Mode
Future job paths can collect education, languages, skills, work preference, availability, city and income goal; identify gaps; build a preparation route; verify skills; and connect to opportunities. Include spoken English, communication, interviews, AI mock interviews, resume/profile, customer support, sales, retail, digital literacy and role-specific practice.

## 20. Expansion Beyond Crochet
Crochet is the launch vertical, not a permanent technical limitation. Future families may include handmade/creative skills, home-income skills, digital skills and career/job-readiness skills.

## 21. Subscription & Support Levels
The same curriculum can support self-learning/recorded, subscription library, group learning, 1:1 teacher support and targeted intervention.

## 22. Five Intelligence Layers
1. **Person** — goal, language, digital comfort, time and learning pace.
2. **Skill** — capability, practice, evidence, gaps and verification.
3. **Teaching** — best next medium: video, AI clip, PDF, group, 1:1, voice or teacher intervention.
4. **Opportunity** — products, services, jobs, teaching and demand.
5. **Life** — time, affordability, connectivity, materials and real-world constraints.

The purpose is fewer decisions for the learner, not more settings.

## 23. Backend Domains to Plan
Teacher profiles/skills/languages/verification/availability; teacher slots; courses/modules/lessons/resources/translations; batches/enrollments; sessions/participants/attendance; demos; 1:1 bookings; learning chat/voice; practice/submissions/feedback; projects/assessments; AI clips/versioning/review; materials/practice kits; subscriptions/entitlements; Skill Passport/evidence; opportunity/output type; existing Learn & Earn HPay reward foundation and future teacher-learning HPay program.

## 24. Build Discipline
1. Reuse HOWDI authentication.
2. Never expose backend complexity to the learner.
3. Do not build a generic course-marketplace UI.
4. Remove avoidable teacher administration.
5. Never auto-publish AI instructional content without review controls.
6. Treat PDF/offline/voice/regional-language as core capabilities.
7. Keep HPay reward eligibility controlled.
8. Keep architecture category-neutral even though crochet launches first.
9. Do not claim live providers/integrations before they exist.
10. Review learner, teacher, Admin and backend impact before structural releases.

## 25. Recommended Build Sequence
- **V19.1 Foundation:** Teacher identity/portal foundation + learning mode/resource model + learner Journey Mode architecture.
- **V19.2 Scheduling:** Availability, demos, group batches, 1:1, session lifecycle and attendance.
- **V19.3 Learner Experience:** One-button learning, Daily Skill Companion, readiness, voice/help, offline/PDF.
- **V19.4 Practice & Proof:** Submissions, feedback, mistake library, final projects, Skill Passport.
- **V19.5 AI Studio:** Teacher Copilot, AI Clip Studio, review/versioning/approval.
- **V19.6 Opportunity Bridge:** Shop/Works/creator/service pathways and controlled HPay connections.
- **Later:** Subscriptions, market-demand intelligence, jobs, SHG/institutional learning and deeper AI vision assistance.

## 26. Final Product Principle
**HOWDI should continuously decide the easiest useful next step for each person.**

The learner sees simplicity. The teacher gets leverage. The Admin gets control. The ecosystem creates opportunity.
